Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery options?

We offer standard delivery (3-5 days) and express delivery (1-2 days) across India. Delivery charges vary by location.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days if the product is in original condition with all tags intact. Return shipping is free.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery, UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and WhatsApp payment options.

How do I track my order?

After order confirmation, we'll send you a tracking link via WhatsApp and email so you can monitor delivery in real-time.

Do you have a size guide?

Yes! We provide detailed size guides for all categories. Use our size chart or contact us on WhatsApp for personalized sizing help.

Can I place bulk orders?

Yes! We offer special bulk discounts for corporate orders and bulk purchases. Contact us at 8838247446 for details.
